07 — VOYAGER IDENTITY
The AI assistant has its own character: a soft white orb with gradient “smiling eyes”. Friendly, calm, never anthropomorphized beyond the eyes — no mouth, no limbs, no emotions other than attentive warmth.
Meet AI Voyager
Orb: white sphere, radial highlight at 35%/30%, soft inner shading (in 3D renders: matte ceramic, studio light). Eyes: two ∩-arcs, stroke ≈ 10% of orb diameter, round caps, one gradient across both — left eye violet #8b5cf6 → right eye blue #2962ff. Wordmark: Plus Jakarta Sans 800 with the same 90° gradient.
