Why Voyager Private is different

Ultimate provides the platform’s most advanced professional tools. Voyager Private adds a persistent private intelligence layer that can understand the user’s approved portfolio, goals, documents, research and financial context.

Unlimited Voyager AI

Run deep analysis and complex research without standard conversational limits, subject to a transparent fair-use policy.

Persistent private context

Voyager retains the approved context throughout the active subscription instead of starting from zero in every session.

Autonomous Deep Research

Voyager can structure complex research into multiple analytical stages and produce a sourced final report.

Private Financial Vault

Use approved reports, statements, spreadsheets and research documents as private Voyager context.

Personal Knowledge Graph

Turn approved goals, assets, research, notes and decisions into a connected visual data graph — Obsidian-style connected notes and relationships.

Portfolio Digital Twin

Model how markets, currencies, rates and liquidity scenarios could affect the user’s financial structure.

Proactive Private Intelligence

Surface important risks, contradictions, portfolio changes and outdated assumptions based on approved private context.

These capabilities are presented as plan contents. Availability is announced separately as each one ships.
